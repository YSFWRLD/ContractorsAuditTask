## Key clauses

Clauses that decide measurement, pricing, records and payment. `verbatim` text is transcribed exactly; `paraphrase` marks a single-pass summary of lower-impact text.

- **1 Definitions** (p.3, verbatim): Words and expressions defined in Appendix A shall bear those meanings throughout. Where a word is used in both the singular and the plural the meaning is unaffected.
- **2 Subcontract documents** (p.3, verbatim): This Agreement, the General Conditions, Schedules 1 to 5 and Appendix A together constitute the whole agreement between the parties. In the event of conflict, a Schedule prevails over the General Conditions, and a later-numbered Schedule prevails over an earlier one.
- **3 Scope of the Works** (p.3, verbatim): The Subcontractor shall execute the civil and earthworks described in Schedule 1 at the locations shown on the Contract Drawings, together with all work reasonably to be inferred as necessary for their completion.
- **4 The Site** (p.3, verbatim): The Site is divided into four zones for the purposes of measurement, described in Schedule 2. The zone applicable to an item of work is the zone in which the work is physically executed, irrespective of where materials are stored or plant is based.
- **5 Ground conditions** (p.3, verbatim): The ground is classified into five categories described in Schedule 3. Classification shall be determined by the Engineer from the trial pit records and confirmed in writing before the work is executed. A classification once confirmed shall not be revised retrospectively.
- **6 Programme** (p.3, verbatim): The Subcontractor shall submit a programme within fourteen days of the Commencement Date and shall update it monthly.
- **7 Working hours** (p.3, verbatim): Normal working hours are 06:00 to 18:00 on working days. Work executed between 18:00 and 06:00 is Night Work and attracts the uplift stated in Schedule 4 where, and only where, that Schedule marks the item as eligible.
- **8 Rest days** (p.3, verbatim): The rest days under this Subcontract are Friday and Saturday. Work executed on a rest day attracts the rest-day uplift stated in Schedule 4 where the item is marked as eligible. No item attracts both the night uplift and the rest-day uplift on the same measurement unless the Engineer has instructed the work in writing under Clause 9.
- **9 Instructed out-of-hours working** (p.3, verbatim): The Engineer may instruct work outside normal working hours. An instruction shall be in writing and shall state whether the night uplift, the rest-day uplift, or both are to apply.
- **10 Care of the Works** (p.3, paraphrase): Subcontractor responsible for care of the Works until the Taking-Over Certificate.
- **11 Setting out** (p.4, verbatim): The Subcontractor shall set out the Works from the primary control stations. Setting out is measured under item E.52.010 by chainage band and only against a joint survey signed by both parties.
- **12 Materials and workmanship** (p.4, verbatim): All materials shall be new and of the qualities described. Materials not described shall be of a quality approved by the Engineer.
- **13 Testing** (p.4, verbatim): The Subcontractor shall carry out the tests described in the Specification. Where Schedule 5 makes a test certificate a condition of payment, no valuation shall include the item until the certificate has been delivered.
- **14 Defective work** (p.4, verbatim): The Engineer may instruct the removal and re-execution of defective work. Re-execution is at the Subcontractor's cost and shall not be measured again.
- **15 Variations** (p.4, verbatim): The Engineer may instruct variations. A varied item shall be valued at the rates in Schedule 1 where the character and conditions of the work are not substantially changed.
- **16 Provisional sums** (p.4, verbatim): Provisional sums shall be expended only on the written instruction of the Engineer and shall be omitted from the Subcontract Sum if not so instructed.
- **17 Dayworks** (p.4, verbatim): Work instructed to be executed on a daywork basis shall be recorded on daywork sheets signed daily by the Engineer's representative. Daywork sheets not signed on the day of the work shall not be admitted.
- **18 Plant and standing time** (p.4, verbatim): Standing time is not payable except (a) where the Works are suspended by reason of adverse weather, in which case item E.51.010 applies against the meteorological record, and (b) plant standing measured under item E.51.030 against the plant standing record, each as required by Schedule 5.
- **19 Traffic management** (p.4, verbatim): Traffic management is measured by the day under item E.51.020. Not more than one day of traffic management is measurable per work area per day, irrespective of the number of locations at which signals are deployed.
- **20 Health and safety** (p.4, verbatim): The Subcontractor shall comply with the Project Health and Safety Plan.
- **21 Insurance** (p.4, verbatim): The Subcontractor shall maintain the insurances described in Appendix A throughout the period of the Works. _Note: Insurances are in fact set out in Appendix C, not Appendix A (cross-reference error)._
- **22 Assignment and subletting** (p.4, paraphrase): No assignment or subletting without consent (continues on page 5).
- **23 Suspension** (p.5, paraphrase): Contractor may suspend the Works on seven days' notice.
- **24 Termination** (p.5, paraphrase): Termination grounds by reference to Appendix A. _Note: Appendix A contains definitions only; no termination grounds are listed there._
- **25 Principle of measurement** (p.6, verbatim): The Works shall be measured net as executed in accordance with the Civil Engineering Standard Method of Measurement, as modified by these Clauses. Where a Clause of this Part conflicts with the Method of Measurement, this Part prevails.
- **26 Units of measurement** (p.6, verbatim): Each item in Schedule 1 states the unit in which it is measured. No item shall be measured in any other unit, and a quantity presented in a unit other than that stated shall be rejected in its entirety rather than converted.
- **27 Build-up of rates** (p.6, verbatim): The rates in Schedule 1 are base rates. The sum payable for a measured quantity is ascertained by applying to the base rate, in the following order and no other: first the site zone factor from Schedule 2; then the ground classification factor from Schedule 3; then the night uplift, if applicable; then the rest-day uplift, if applicable; then the cumulative quantity rebate from Schedule 4, if the threshold has been passed; and the resulting sum is then rounded.
- **28 Rounding** (p.6, verbatim): Rounding shall be applied once only, to the rate after the whole of the build-up described in Clause 27 has been completed, to the nearest halala, a half halala being rounded upward. Rates shall not be rounded at intermediate steps. The amount for a measured quantity is the quantity multiplied by the rounded rate; where Clause 30 divides a quantity at a rebate threshold, each part is multiplied by its own rounded rate and the amount is their sum.
- **29 Application of factors** (p.6, verbatim): A factor applies to an item only where Schedule 2 or Schedule 3 lists that item's series. An item whose series is not listed is measured at the base rate irrespective of the zone or ground classification in which it is executed.
- **30 Cumulative quantity rebates** (p.6, verbatim): Where Schedule 4 states a rebate threshold for an item, the rebate applies to that part of the quantity which exceeds the threshold when taken cumulatively across the whole of the Works to date, and not to the quantity below it. The cumulative count runs from the Commencement Date, is not reset by a valuation, and is taken in order of the date of execution; measurements executed on the same date are taken in order of application number and then line number. _Note: Schedule 4 Part 3 states 'Clause 30 is substituted' and counts bands per Contract Year (Clause 3A). See ambiguity AMB-BANDS._
- **31 Daily limitations** (p.6, verbatim): Where Schedule 4 states a daily limitation for an item, no more than that quantity is measurable for that item, per work area, per day. A quantity in excess of the limitation is not payable and shall not be carried forward to a subsequent day.
- **32 Mutually exclusive items** (p.6, verbatim): Where Schedule 4 states that an item is excluded by another, the excluded item is not measurable within the stated number of days following the measurement of the excluding item on the same work area.
- **33 Surveyed items** (p.7, verbatim): Items marked as surveyed in Schedule 1 are measured only against a joint survey sheet signed by both parties. The quantity measured shall be the quantity on the survey sheet exactly. _Note: Schedule 1 carries no 'surveyed' marker; surveyed items are listed in Schedule 4 Part 6. Clause 33A later adds a 2 per cent tolerance._
- **34 Provisional quantities** (p.7, verbatim): Quantities in Schedule 1 are estimates and do not limit the quantity that may be measured.
- **35 Work not measurable** (p.7, verbatim): Temporary works, dewatering below the classified water table, and the removal of the Subcontractor's own waste are deemed included in the rates and are not separately measurable.
- **36 Disputed measurement** (p.7, verbatim): Where the parties cannot agree a measurement, the Engineer's measurement shall be used for the purposes of interim valuation without prejudice.
- **37 Re-measurement** (p.7, verbatim): The Contractor may re-measure any item within ninety days of the valuation in which it was first included.
