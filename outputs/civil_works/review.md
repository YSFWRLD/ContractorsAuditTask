## 9. Reviews of the unresolved readings

### Indexation (Schedule 2A, Clause 29A, Appendix B)

- Schedule 2A (p.21): "priced by reference to the Site Materials Index, base 100.00 at 5 January 2025 (Clause 29A). The rate stated in Schedule 1 for these items is a base rate and is not payable as it stands."
- Clause 29A (p.32): "The rate for an item listed in Schedule 2A is the base rate stated in Schedule 1 multiplied by the Site Materials Index published for the calendar month in which the work was executed and divided by the base index, and rounded to the nearest halala with a half rounded to the nearer even halala. That figure is then treated as the rate for the purposes of Clause 27, and Clause 28 applies to the build-up as usual."
- Automatic: yes (the Schedule 1 rate 'is not payable as it stands'). External data: none — the Site Materials Index is printed for every month 2025-01 to 2026-12 and the base is 100.00. Compounding: no (each month is taken against the fixed base). Effect: it replaces the unit rate before the Clause 27 build-up.
- Against it: the Appendix B worked example prices C.31.010 on 13/05/2025 at 86.20 x 1.06 = 91.37, unindexed.
- Weight: Clause 2 makes 'Schedules 1 to 5 and Appendix A' the whole agreement; Schedule 2A sits within Schedules 1 to 5, Appendix B does not. That favours 29A more strongly than recorded in Phase 2, but the conflict is inside the document, so the reading stays UNRESOLVED (MEDIUM) and is not upgraded in this freeze.
- Alternative effect: 279 lines, 237 totals, 215 unflagged applications would be flagged, 0 findings lost. Production reading (29A) remains justified.

### Monthly rates (S1 1.2 for D.41.020, A2 2.2 for E.54.010)

- Wording: 'The rate payable for a measured quantity is the rate published for the calendar month in which the work was executed. Where work is executed in a month later than the last month stated below, the rate last published applies until it is superseded.'
- Payable amount = quantity x rate; no daily or pro-rata treatment is stated; the month of the work date selects the rate.
- Open: whether zone factor / night uplift apply on top (base rate) or not (final rate). E.54.010 is Series E with no uplift, band or discount, so only D.41.020 (May-November 2025) is affected.
- Alternative effect: 40 lines, 37 totals, 33 unflagged applications would be flagged, 0 findings lost. Stays UNRESOLVED (MEDIUM).

### Backdated adjustment (Amendment No. 3, Clause 31A)

- Clause 31A (p.32): "Where an Amendment substitutes a rate with effect from a date before the date on which it was issued, an Application for Payment submitted before that date of issue is not thereby incorrect: it was valued at the rate then in force. The difference between the rates on work already valued shall be shown as a single adjustment on the first Application for Payment submitted on or after the date of issue, and on no other."
- Trigger: A3 issued 2026-05-12; effective 2025-11-01 (retrospective). The difference is 'a single adjustment' on one application, i.e. the `adjustment` column, not `application_total` (Clause 45A: payable = measured total + 31A adjustment - retention + release).
- Open: 'on or after' the issue date (31A) vs 'after' (A3 recital). Three applications are dated 2026-05-12.
- Alternative effect: 2 lines, 2 totals, 2 unflagged applications would be flagged, 1 findings lost. PA-00006 carries the finding under the working reading, PA-00443 under the alternative; the finding is LOW.

### Retention and its release

- Retention (Clause 45) and the release (Clause 45A) are separate columns (`retention`, `retention_released`), outside `application_total`.
- PA-00678, the first application after 2026-09-30, shows no release: a contractual error (`adjustment_missing`) whose correction does not change `application_total` (`total_status` says whether the total itself is wrong).

## 11. Primary category precedence

The row's `error_category` is the finding with the lowest (tier, category precedence, line, rule). Monetary size is never used.

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | explicit contractual violation, HIGH confidence, that changes the application total |
| 2 | any other finding that changes the application total |
| 3 | determinable sum settled outside the total (adjustment, release, retention) |
| 4 | structural / data-integrity issue without a monetary effect |
| 5 | timing or documentation only |

Within a tier: work_after_final_completion > work_outside_contract_period > duplicate_line > duplicate_record > missing_record > item_record_mismatch > record_line_mismatch > unsigned_record > item_not_in_contract > unit_mismatch > line_outside_application_period > quantity_exceeds_record > daily_limit_exceeded > exclusion_window_violation > unit_rate_mismatch > rebate_incorrectly_applied > discount_incorrectly_applied > line_total_arithmetic > application_total_mismatch > adjustment_missing > adjustment_incorrectly_applied > retention_incorrect > contract_reference_mismatch > application_period_misstated > application_timing.
