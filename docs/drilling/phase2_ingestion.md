# Drilling Phase 2: ingestion

Phase 2 turns the raw drilling files into typed, traceable objects, indexes and timelines, plus a structural validation report. It stops before anything contractual: no service identification, no pricing, no findings.

## Source files

Two CSVs and 8,151 report files; see `phase2_data_inventory.md`. The contract PDF is handled by Phase 1.

## Parser design (`src/contractor_audit/domains/drilling/ingestion/`)

| module | role |
|---|---|
| `models.py` | frozen dataclasses: `DrillingInvoice`, `InvoiceLine`, `DailyReport` (Parts A–E, `Signature`, `CrewEntry`, `RawField`), `ParseIssue`, `SourceRef`, `DrillingDataset` |
| `values.py` | strict value parsers: dates (DD-Mon-YYYY, independent of locale), whole numbers, Decimals, money to integer cents (exactly two decimals; never rounds), Yes/No |
| `invoices.py` | `invoices.csv` / `invoice_lines.csv`; a wrong header or ragged row raises `IngestionError`; a bad cell becomes `None` plus a `ParseIssue` |
| `reports.py` | schema-driven parser for the report layout; anomalies become `ParseIssue`s |
| `loaders.py` | `load_dataset(data_root)`: everything, with source fingerprints |
| `indexes.py` | `KeyIndex` (key → every member; `one()` raises on a duplicate) and `DrillingIndexes` |
| `timelines.py` | `WellTimeline` and `BhaRun` reconstruction |
| `validation.py` | `validate(dataset)` → `StructuralReport` of neutral `Observation`s |

**Typing rules:**
- Money is integer cents (`net_cents`, `amount_cents`, …).
- Quantities and unit rates are `Decimal`.
- Dates are `datetime.date`.
- Hours, depths and counts are `int`.
- No float is produced anywhere.

**What is kept as stated:**
- the well class is `well_class_stated`, exactly as printed;
- the service code is the contractor's code;
- contract-reference variants are not corrected;
- tools and crew roles are the rig's words, in printed order.

**Traceability:**
- Every invoice and line keeps its raw CSV row (`raw`) and a `SourceRef` (file, physical line, identifier).
- Every report keeps each `Label: value` line as a `RawField` with its part and physical line, the file it came from, and its `ParseIssue`s.

**Failing safely:**
- The report parser records each of these as an issue and sets the value to `None`: unknown sections, unknown labels, duplicated labels (the first value is kept), blank values, non key-value lines, missing parts or fields, malformed numbers or dates, malformed crew entries, fields after the signatures, blank signatures, and unreadable or non-`.txt` files.
- Nothing is defaulted.
- On the real data the only parse issues are the 6 blank signature lines (INFO).

## Report variants

There are six combinations of parts: A+B always, plus C, D and/or E. All reports use one template and the same labels, and the signature block is one trailing block. See the inventory for the counts.

## Timelines and BHA runs

- **`WellTimeline`:**
  - the rigs, fields and stated classes;
  - reports ordered by date, with first and last dates and any gaps;
  - invoice periods, which of them overlap, report days not covered by any period, and period days without a report.
- **`BhaRun`:**
  - the recorded first and last days (every distinct value, never collapsed) against the observed first and last report;
  - the report ids and whether they are consecutive;
  - tools, sections, stated run hours against the sum of daily hours;
  - the discrepancies.

On the real data: 214 wells with no gaps, and 1,369 runs with no discrepancies.

## Structural validation

`artifacts/drilling/phase2_structural_validation.{json,md}` is regenerated by `build-artifacts --domain drilling`. It holds the counts, the variants, one entry per observation, a summary by code, per-well timelines and run statistics.

**Where it is written.** The prompt suggested `outputs/drilling/`. The report is written to `artifacts/` instead because:
- it is a descriptive profile, like the Civil Works source profiles;
- `outputs/` holds audit results;
- a frozen Civil Works test asserts that no `outputs/drilling*` exists yet.

No contractual window, rate or rule is applied. The invoice-date lag is reported as a distribution (min −2, median 17, max 55 days), not judged against Clause 33.

**Evidence for open Phase 1 ambiguities.** The JSON's `ambiguity_evidence` section, written by `reporting/ambiguity_evidence.py`, records facts for AMB-05/06/12/13/14/15/17/20/22/23/24/25 and decides none of them. For example:
- the Part E hours equal the lost tool's own accumulated daily circulating hours in 54 of 54 reports, but the whole-well total in only 27;
- metres logged and reamed equal the run's depth advance wherever they are non-zero;
- the adjustment column is always 0.00.

This module is the only Phase 2 code that reads a contract fact: the back-dated instrument's issue date, taken from the contract model.

## Known data-quality issues

- **The three contract-reference variants.**
- **The report cited by two invoices.** MDS-01352 cites DDR-063-20260427, a date outside its own period, for an LW-411 line.
- **Eight service dates that differ from their report's date.** Six of them are also outside their invoice period (three) or dated in 2027 (three); the other two are one day off.
- **Three invoice periods extended into 2027.**
- **Recorded arithmetic that doesn't add up:** three totals that differ from net + VAT, and three line amounts that differ from quantity × rate.
- **Three unsigned reports.**
- **One gyro count without its Part C.**

Each is an observation for the audit phases, not a finding.

## What Phase 2 intentionally does not do

- It does not map rig words (or anything else) to contract service codes. Appendix G is used only in Phase 3, from the contract model.
- It does not price, apply factors or discounts, or check rates.
- It does not decide eligibility (call-offs, contract term, performance sections) or the contractual effect of signatures, dates or periods.
- It generates no finding, no `InvoiceResult`, nothing in `outputs/drilling`, and no submission.
- It resolves no Phase 1 ambiguity.

Tests in `tests/drilling/test_phase2_boundaries.py` enforce the first, second and fourth points.
